How it works

  • 01Quarterly threshold. Star Plans' total sales for the quarter are measured against the $110,000 line.
  • 02Everything above it counts. Akiva earns 5% of whatever sales close above that number — not the full quarter's total.
  • 03Paid per quarter. This isn't a year-end bonus — it's calculated and paid out at the close of each quarter, so the upside is felt right away.
  • 04Grows as the company grows. There's no cap. As Star Plans scales past $110K by more each quarter, this plan scales with it.
  • 05Revisited, not set in stone. After 12 months, we sit down together, look at how it's performed, and get Akiva's feedback before deciding what carries forward.
